Set up Stockwell on this PC

Choose once. The books live on one PC (the main PC). The other PC opens them over the office network, the same way TallyPrime works with two PCs.

Changing the main PC? First make a backup on the old main PC (Gateway → Backup), choose "This is the main PC" here, then restore that backup here (Backup → Restore). The books already on this PC are never deleted by this page.
